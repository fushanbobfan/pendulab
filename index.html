Total angular offset between the first and last pendulum, on a log scale.

Drag either white bob to pose the pendulum. Changing an angle, mass, length, gravity, damping, count or spread re-releases the pendulums from rest. Shortcuts: Space pause, R release again, T trails, [/] previous or next preset.

The white pendulum is the reference. Every coloured one starts a fraction of a degree away from it. For small swings they move as one; raise the bobs and the fan tears apart within seconds, and the chart shows the separation climbing along a straight line on the log axis: exponential growth, the signature of chaos.
